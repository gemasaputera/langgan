# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Indonesian consumers managing personal digital subscriptions — Netflix, Spotify, mobile data, cloud storage, etc. They check the app casually: before payday, when a bill feels off, or when considering a new subscription. Primary context is mobile, brief sessions, often while multitasking.

## Product Purpose

Langgan helps Indonesians see the full picture of their recurring spending in IDR. It tracks due dates, calculates monthly and yearly totals, and sends payment reminders so users never get surprised by an auto-debit again. Success looks like: a user opening the app, immediately understanding where their money goes, and feeling in control rather than anxious.

## Brand Personality

Warm, friendly, approachable. Financial wellness with a human touch: adult and responsible, but never cold or corporate. Think "a helpful friend who happens to be great with spreadsheets," not a bank teller or a cartoon mascot.

## Anti-references

- **Cartoonish / childish aesthetics** (Duolingo-level playfulness, excessive illustrations, bubbly shapes). Friendly is fine; juvenile is not.
- **Dense banking dashboards** (dark-gray data grids, tiny numbers, overwhelming tables). This is personal finance, not trading.
- **Generic SaaS template look** (identical icon + heading + text card grids, gradient hero text, "streamline / empower / supercharge" copy). The category reflex must be avoided.
- **Overly serious fintech palettes** (navy + gold, all-caps labels, rigid hierarchy). The user is managing Spotify, not a stock portfolio.

## Design Principles

1. **Financial clarity without anxiety.** Numbers should feel manageable, not overwhelming. Use rounding, plain language, and context ("Rp 180rb/bulan = 2 tiket bioskop") to make costs relatable.
2. **Warm minimalism.** Clean and uncluttered, but never cold. Avoid pure grayscale; tint neutrals toward a warm hue. Rounded corners and soft shadows over sharp edges and heavy borders.
3. **Mobile-first for casual check-ins.** Most sessions are brief and on-the-go. Prioritize thumb-zone layout, large touch targets, and instant comprehension. The dashboard should answer "how much and when" in under 3 seconds.
4. **Local context matters.** IDR formatting (Rp 150.000, not Rp150000), Indonesian language throughout, and awareness of local subscription patterns (GoPay, OVO, Telkomsel, etc.).
5. **Trust through transparency.** Show exactly what the user pays for and when. No hidden fees, no dark patterns, no ambiguity in billing cycles.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance for all contrast, keyboard navigation, and screen-reader support.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: animations become instant transitions or crossfades.
- Color is never the sole indicator of state (supplemented with icons, text labels, or patterns).
- Touch targets minimum 44×44dp on mobile.
